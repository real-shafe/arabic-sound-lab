Contrasts that change meaning

Each card is a minimal pair: two words that differ by one sound. Play both back to back and listen for the single difference.

Vowels: only three, but length matters

Arabic has three vowel qualities, each short or long, plus two diphthongs. Short vowels are usually left unwritten, so reading depends on knowing the word.

The vowel a changes color next to emphatics

Sun and moon letters

The l of the article ال "the" blends into a following consonant made with the tongue tip (sun letters), which then doubles. Before other consonants (moon letters) the l is pronounced. The spelling never changes.
